## Findings

during startup/title flow. Temporarily setting Windows to 2012-09-30
allows normal boot and gameplay. Autosave Off and Living Rosters Off did
not eliminate the date-dependent freeze.
Graphics/audio/controller/gameplay/save-load pass after the date
workaround.
